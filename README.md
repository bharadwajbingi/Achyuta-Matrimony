# Achyuta Matrimony: Trusted Telugu Connections

I want you to DESIGN AND BUILD a premium, production-quality LANDING PAGE for my new matrimonial business.

IMPORTANT:

Do not make a generic AI-looking wedding website.

Do not copy the UI, branding, text, illustrations, layout, colors, or identity of any existing matrimonial company.

Before designing, research the current websites and landing-page patterns of major Indian matrimonial services, especially:

- Shaadi.com

- BharatMatrimony

- TeluguMatrimony

- Telugu/community-focused matrimonial services

- premium Indian family/lifestyle brands

Study what makes their websites easy to understand, trustworthy, mobile-friendly and conversion-focused.

Then take the BEST UX principles from them and create an ORIGINAL identity and landing page for my brand.

MOST IMPORTANT BUSINESS GOAL

==================================================

This is an MVP landing page.

I am NOT building a full matchmaking platform yet.

The actual business process is:

Facebook / Instagram advertisement

        ↓

Achyuta Matrimony landing page

        ↓

        ↓

Visitor registers interest

        ↓

Google Form / registration form

OR

WhatsApp Business

        ↓

I personally call the family

        ↓

Explain membership

        ↓

Payment

        ↓

        ↓

Therefore the website's primary job is:

3. Make Telugu families comfortable

4. Get the visitor to register

5. Get the visitor to contact us on WhatsApp

DO NOT build unnecessary login systems, matchmaking algorithms, profile databases, dashboards or complicated features.

The landing page should make a visitor think:

"This looks like a genuine Telugu matrimonial service. I can trust them enough to register and talk to them."

==================================================

The website must be extremely easy for ordinary Telugu-speaking families to understand.

Do NOT use complicated English.

Primary visual language:

Telugu + simple English.

Use Telugu prominently in important sections and CTAs where appropriate.

For example:

మీ కుమారుడు / కుమార్తె కోసం సరైన సంబంధాన్ని వెతకండి

Do NOT translate everything awkwardly.

Use natural, modern Telugu.

The page should still work for users comfortable with English.

Consider a small language switch:

but do not make the interface complicated.
